# FAQ

**Is it free?** Yes. Karobar ERP is open source under the MIT license.

**Is it ready for my shop?** Almost. Every module works and has tests; the last milestone adds
end-to-end tests and the public demo before `v1.0.0`. Try the [[Demo]] first. Follow the
[roadmap](https://github.com/ahmershahdev/karobar-erp/blob/main/docs/roadmap.md).

**Which countries does it support?** Pakistan, the UAE and Saudi Arabia. Sales tax and VAT rates
are entered as dated data. Payroll knows Pakistan's income tax slabs and the UAE and Saudi
gratuity rules; see [[Payroll]] for what you enter yourself.

**Can it print bills?** Yes: six bill designs with your logo and signature, on A4 or an 80 mm till
roll, in English, Urdu or Arabic. A bill can also be shared as a link or on WhatsApp.

**Does the AI chat see my data?** Only if the server owner adds a Groq key. It then sends the
question and the figures your role may see to Groq to write the answer, and never posts, pays or
deletes anything.

**Can I use it offline?** No. Karobar runs on a server and the browser needs to reach it.

**Where do I ask questions?** In [Discussions](https://github.com/ahmershahdev/karobar-erp/discussions).
