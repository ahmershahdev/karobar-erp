# FAQ

**Is it free?** Yes. Karobar ERP is open source under the MIT license.

**Is it ready for my shop?** Not yet. The platform is in place; inventory, sales and accounting
are being built. Follow the [roadmap](https://github.com/ahmershahdev/karobar-erp/blob/main/docs/roadmap.md).

**Which countries' taxes does it support?** Tax rates are stored as data with dates, so any rate
can be entered. Pakistan comes first, then the Gulf countries.

**Can I use it offline?** No. Karobar runs on a server and the browser needs to reach it.

**Where do I ask questions?** In [Discussions](https://github.com/ahmershahdev/karobar-erp/discussions).
