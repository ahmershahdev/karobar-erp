# Code of Conduct

Karobar ERP is built in the open by people with different languages, backgrounds and levels of
experience. We want taking part to be a good experience for all of them.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in their second language.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. Explain your reasoning and listen to theirs.
- Give and accept feedback on code gracefully. Reviews are about the change, never the person.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Think about the people who will use this software: shopkeepers, accountants, cashiers.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Discrimination based on age, body, disability, ethnicity, caste, gender identity or
  expression, experience, nationality, religion, sexual orientation or anything similar.
- Publishing anyone's private information, including real customer, supplier or employee data.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or reviews.

## Where it applies

In every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, the wiki, and anywhere someone
represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Email [support@ahmershah.dev](mailto:support@ahmershah.dev) with "Conduct" in the subject. Reports
are read by the maintainer, Syed Ahmer Shah, and kept confidential. You'll get an answer within 7
days. If the report is about the maintainer, say so in the subject and it will be handled with
that conflict in mind.

## Consequences

Depending on what happened, the maintainer may ask for a correction, give a private or public
warning, or ban someone from the project temporarily or for good. The person reported will hear
which rule they broke.
